# Chapter 14: Liberty and Prosperity (Life after the Supreme Court)

Chapter 14 covers Art Panganiban's life from his constitutionally mandated retirement at midnight of his sixty-ninth year (December 2006) through his ongoing post-Supreme Court life as foundation founder, independent director, church benefactor, and elder statesman. It opens with the discipline of his eleven-year tenure: inheriting seven hundred pending cases, being 'unloaded' two hundred and eighty more by senior colleagues, taking thirty-five fresh cases each month, and yet meeting a self-imposed quota of 104 decisions per year (two a week) calibrated to the 1987 Constitution's twenty-four-month deadline. As Chief Justice, with seventy percent of his time going to administration, he still produces two ponencias a week, leaving fifty-nine volumes of Resolutions, Decisions and Separate Opinions -- averaging ten pages a day, seven days a week, for eleven years -- and no case left undecided. The chapter articulates his judicial philosophy, 'to safeguard the liberty and nurture the prosperity of our people under the rule of law,' with its two-track structure: in civil-liberties cases the scales tilt against the government and toward the poor and marginalized; in conflicts affecting prosperity and economic development, deference belongs to the political branches. The chapter then turns to legacy. None of his five children -- all of them now accomplished professionals abroad and at home -- chose law; so he creates the Foundation for Liberty & Prosperity (FLP) to pass on the philosophy: professorial chairs, law scholarships, MBA scholarships, dissertation-writing contests, an educational assistance program for the underprivileged, and two 'ultimate' projects -- a Center and Museum of Liberty and Prosperity, and a multi-billion-peso Fund for Entrepreneurship for MSMEs without access to traditional finance. Outside FLP, leading conglomerates (Meralco, PLDT, BPI, Metrobank, Petron, GMA Network, JG Summit, Jollibee, the Metro Pacific and Robinsons groups, Double Dragon, Merry Mart, Asian Terminals) recruit him as independent director -- a role he treats as judicial impartiality transposed into the boardroom. The Church taps him to lead the Manila Cathedral-Basilica Foundation; with Cardinal Rosales then Archbishop Tagle, and a network of donors, he raises the funds to retrofit and restrengthen the cathedral on liquefaction-prone reclaimed land -- the eighth iteration of the basilica since 1571 -- in three years. The chapter closes with the FLP-mounted Ageless Passion musical for his seventy-fifth and eightieth birthdays, with Ryan Cayabyab's eighteen original songs and the Manila Symphony Orchestra, telecast in full on GMA Network: a public, lyrical summary of a life whose seeming defeats turned, again and again, into successes.

## Notable Anecdotes

### Anecdote 1

On Day One as Associate Justice in October 1995, Art finds 700 pending cases in his predecessor's chamber and is 'unloaded' 280 more by the fourteen senior sitting members -- plus 35 raffled to him each month.

### Anecdote 2

He imposes on himself a quota of 104 written decisions a year (two per week) and uses the 1987 Constitution's 24-month deadline as the measuring stick -- eventually clearing the inherited backlog and reaching only-current cases on his desk.

### Anecdote 3

As Chief Justice, 70 percent of his waking hours go to administration and only 30 percent to writing -- yet he still produces two ponencias a week, ending with 59 volumes of Resolutions, Decisions and Separate Opinions, each about 600 pages: ten pages a day, seven days a week, for over eleven years.

### Anecdote 4

The Manila Cathedral's belfry sways whenever cargo trucks rumble past; the 'as-completed' plans cannot be found in the Archdiocese, the National Library, or the Manila City Engineer's office; the cathedral sits on reclaimed land that could liquify. Art, as foundation president, advises Archbishop Tagle of legal liability if a death follows the engineers' written warning -- Tagle closes the cathedral, and Art's business network funds a complete three-year retrofit (the eighth iteration of the basilica since 1571).

### Anecdote 5

Ageless Passion -- the musical tribute mounted by FLP for his 75th and 80th birthdays -- features eighteen original songs by National Artist Ryan Cayabyab, a libretto by Kristian Jeff Agustin, direction by Bart Guingona, a cast including Michael Williams, Topper Fabregas and Cris Villonco, and accompaniment by the seventy-five-member Manila Symphony Orchestra; GMA Network telecasts the full two hours.

### Anecdote 6

Each of his five children carries multiple postgraduate degrees from top international universities (UC Irvine, Stanford, U. Chicago, U. Michigan, BU, Harvard) and now leads in business, finance, music, banking, finance teaching, and investment management -- fulfilling the promise of education he could not get for himself when he lost the UP slot to a fifteen-centavo fare or the Yale slot to an airplane ticket.

### Anecdote 7

Sixteen-plus blue-chip companies bring him in as independent director after retirement -- Meralco, PLDT, BPI, Metrobank, Petron, GMA Network, First Philippine Holdings, JG Summit, Jollibee, the Metro Pacific group, the Robinsons group, Double Dragon, Merry Mart, Asian Terminals -- letting him recover the social and intellectual life he had set aside on the bench, and channel resources toward charitable causes.
