# Justice and Faith -- Ch. 7: Faith in God and in Ourselves

Chapter 7 of 'Justice and Faith' is CJP's commencement address at the University of Iloilo on March 21, 1997, accepting the LL.D. honoris causa conferred by his long-time friend Congressman Alberto J. Lopez. Rather than 'a pompous lecture,' CJP tells the graduates his story -- Sampaloc poverty, newsboy, bootblack, peddling cigarettes and bibles; Mapa High honors and a UP scholarship he could not take up because his father lacked the 15-centavo bus fare from Cataluña St. to Diliman, so he enrolled at FEU instead 'walking distance from our house'; the youngest FEUCSO presidency and NUSP co-founding where he met Leni Carpio of St. Scholastica's; Salonga's mentorship and the top-ten bar in 1960; an admission to Yale Law that he could not take up for the same fare reason; his Hong Kong wealth years swept away in the 1982 stock-market crash; the 1983 Rotary Manila induction at which he met then-PC chief Gen. Fidel V. Ramos who, twelve years later, appointed him to the Supreme Court after seven JBC bypassings. From this arc CJP distills three lessons. First, adversities are preludes to greater victories -- FEU instead of UP brought Salonga; the denied Yale fare produced Baron Travel and funded five children's overseas master's degrees; the Hong Kong crash freed him to return home and reach the Supreme Court ('an achievement that money cannot buy nor can any stock market crash take away'). Second, do not lose heart -- Anthony Robbins's tabulated age-list of Abraham Lincoln's defeats (31, 32, 34, 35, 36, 43, 46, 48, 55, 56, 58) ends with the US presidency at 60. Third, have faith in God -- Isaiah 55:8-9 ('my thoughts are not your thoughts...my ways are not your ways') and Romans 8:28 ('God makes all things work for the good of those who love Him'). He closes with the four-act farmer's-horse parable ('good luck, bad luck, who knows? Only God knows!') and the synthesizing maxim: 'We will work as if everything depended on us. And we will pray as if everything depended on God.'

## Notable Anecdotes

### Anecdote 1

Doctor-of-Laws-honoris-causa joke -- Leni and all five children hold overseas master's degrees; until today CJP was the only one in the family without a postgraduate degree, until UI 'finally' gave him one

### Anecdote 2

The 15-centavo bus-fare anecdote -- Mapa High honors → UP scholarship → could not enroll because father lacked the bus fare from Cataluña St. in Sampaloc to UP Diliman → FEU instead because it was walking distance

### Anecdote 3

Selling bibles to FEU professors for pocket money; reselling textbooks to classmates to earn a book in commission

### Anecdote 4

Newsboy diary entry: 'Today, I am a newsboy. But someday, I'll be president of a major newspaper' -- fulfilled at PDI in March 1991

### Anecdote 5

Hong Kong 1982 -- first-class travel, yachts, Rolls Royces, Piaget watches, mansions; total loss in the crash; suicide reports cited as context for CJP's heartbreak

### Anecdote 6

1983 Rotary Manila -- CJP meets then-PC chief Gen. Fidel V. Ramos who, 'as fate would have it,' becomes President in 1992 and appoints CJP to the Supreme Court in 1995

### Anecdote 7

Anthony Robbins's tabulated age-list of Lincoln's defeats (31, 32, 34, 35, 36, 43, 46, 48, 55, 56, 58) culminating at President at 60

### Anecdote 8

Farmer's-horse parable in four acts -- runaway → nine wild horses → broken leg → war exemption -- punctuated by the refrain 'good luck, bad luck, who knows?'

### Anecdote 9

Family roll-call of children's overseas master's degrees as the bypassed-Yale compensation -- UC Berkeley, U. Chicago, U. Navarre, U. Michigan, Erasmus Holland, Boston U., Harvard Kennedy, Munich, Stanford (Engineering Economic Systems)
