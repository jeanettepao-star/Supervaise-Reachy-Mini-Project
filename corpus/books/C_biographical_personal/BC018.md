# Justice and Faith -- Ch. 11: Invocation

Chapter 11 of 'Justice and Faith' is a short Invocation -- a public occasional prayer in standard Trinitarian doxological form -- that asks the Holy Spirit to enable an unnamed honoree to continue emulating Jesus, the Beloved Son: humble, upright, obedient, and faithful to the Father's word and will. It petitions for the practical gifts of good health, peace of mind, and 'many more years of service' to God and to the people, and closes with the standard Catholic doxology: 'in the name of Your Son, Jesus, who lives and rules with You in the unity of the Holy Spirit, one God forever and ever. Amen.' Unlike the longer discursive chapters of the volume, this one is intentionally brief and liturgical -- it serves as the pivot to the Appendix that follows.
