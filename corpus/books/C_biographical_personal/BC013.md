# Justice and Faith -- Ch. 6: Faith Brought Me to the Supreme Court

Chapter 6 of 'Justice and Faith' is CJP's BLD prayer-meeting reflection a week after he took his October 10, 1995 oath as Justice of the Supreme Court, recounting how Scripture and prayer guided him through three years of frustration and seven JBC bypassings 1992-1995 (alleged insufficiency of competence, a former client surfacing with a 25-year-old complaint, the 'career CA justice' priority, the 'others are even more qualified' formula) -- sustained throughout by Romans 8:28. In January 1995 CJP surrendered: 'Take, O Lord, all that I am, all that I will ever be.' When Justice Quiason retired in July, a close-friend justice nudged him; on Saturday July 29 at 1 a.m. CJP could not sleep and prayed for two hours, asleep at 4 a.m. awaiting a sign. The sign came at Mass the next morning -- Luke 11:1-11, 'Ask, seek, knock' -- with cold perspiration and hair on end. He asked for confirmation; on Sunday August 6, the transfiguration gospel (Luke 9:28-36) supplied 'Listen to Him.' He resubmitted his name to the JBC. On Wednesday August 9, with wife Leni as co-prayer-leader at the BLD South Sector meeting at St. James School in Sucat, Parañaque, CJP boldly proclaimed the discernment publicly -- at the risk of ridicule if it proved false -- quoting the Jesuit maxim 'I will work as if everything depended on me, and I will pray as if everything depended on God.' At the JBC's September 13 meeting an exclusion attempt was deferred when one member (CJP believes guided by the Holy Spirit) pleaded for a postponement; on September 20 'no one, by some mysterious reason, objected,' and CJP passed the screening for the first time. Two weeks of intercession by the BLD community, the Carmelites and the Pink Sisters followed, then the appointment call. President Ramos asked, 'How did you do it?' -- CJP replied, 'I did not do it. The Lord did it!' He closes by pledging before the assembly that 'all my actions and all my decisions will only be in accordance with His commandments and His gospel. I hold office by God's grace and I pledge to serve Him and our people with fortitude, integrity, competence and prudence.'

## Notable Anecdotes

### Anecdote 1

The seven bypassings 1992-1995 -- including a former client surfacing with a 25-year-old complaint and the 'others are even more qualified' formula; CJP's sobbed prayer 'How long, O Lord, must I bear this frustration and embarrassment of being rejected?'

### Anecdote 2

The 1 a.m. vigil of July 29, 1995 -- CJP cannot sleep, prays over two hours, falls asleep at 4 a.m. awaiting a sign; the sign comes the next morning at Mass with Luke 11:1-11

### Anecdote 3

The August 6 confirmation -- three words 'Listen to Him' from the transfiguration gospel; CJP's body-hair stands up and cold sweat oozes 'all over my skin'

### Anecdote 4

August 9 BLD South Sector at St. James School, Sucat -- CJP and Leni as prayer leaders; CJP boldly proclaims the discernment at risk of ridicule; quotes the Jesuit 'work as if everything depended on me, pray as if everything depended on God'

### Anecdote 5

September 13, 1995 JBC near-exclusion -- an unnamed member pleads, 'I think we should let Panganiban's name stay in the list in the meantime. We are meeting next week anyway' -- and that pleading is later read as the Holy Spirit's hand

### Anecdote 6

President Ramos's incredulous call after the JBC list arrives -- 'How did you do it?' -- and CJP's reply, 'I did not do it. The Lord did it!'

### Anecdote 7

Intercessory mobilization -- CJP asks his BLD community at every prayer meeting; Leni asks the Carmelites and Pink Sisters; the community is told to 'storm the heavens with supplications and sacrifices'
