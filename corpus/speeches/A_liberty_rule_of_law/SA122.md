# Local Solutions to National Problems

Keynote address of retired Chief Justice Artemio V. Panganiban during the opening of the World Bank-sponsored Philippine Development Innovation Marketplace, Panibagong Paraan 2008, held on April 9, 2008 at the SM Megamall, Mandaluyong City.

---

I thank World Bank Country Director Bert Hofman for inviting me to be the Keynote Speaker during the opening of the Philippine Development Innovation Marketplace, Panibagong Paraan 2008. I am very impressed by Panibagong Paraan's search for innovative ideas as well as by the theme of this year's program, Building Partnerships for Local Governance, that I decided to accept the challenge of facing you today.

A COUNTRY IN CRISIS

Let me begin my keynote with an assessment of the present state of governance in our country. The CBCP said that we are in the midst of restlessness and confusion, facing a crisis of truth and the pervading cancer of corruption. We must seek the truth and we must restore integrity. The Catholic Church asked the faithful to go back to circles of discernment at the grassroots level, in our parishes, Basic Ecclesial Communities, recognized lay organizations and movements. From the grassroots will come out a culture of truth and integrity we so deeply seek and build.

GRASSROOTS SOLUTIONS

Today in our Panibagong Paraan search for innovative ideas for development, we also turn to local governance for cooperative solutions to our national problems. Instead of using spiritual discernment, prayer and communal action, we look into more secular concepts and processes to promote and perpetuate the same goals of integrity, accountability and transparency in governance.

Also, instead of the usual Filipino method of solving problems from the top down, we invert the organizational pyramid by starting good governance innovations from the base going up to the apex of government. This great innovation of solving the nation's problems from the base up rather than the traditional direction coming from the top going to the grassroots is itself the big innovation in our marketplace today.

PRINCIPLES AND VALUES

Let me now share with you some principles and values that will help us in our search for ideas to solve our nation's ills. First, uphold truth and integrity at all times. Truth is the basis of credibility that in turn is the indispensable ingredient of successful governance. Integrity goes beyond mere honesty or the refusal to tell a falsehood; it is the moral courage to denounce a wrong and to promote the truth.

Second, practice transparency and exact accountability. Those who govern must be ready to endure the sunlight and the scrutiny of their public as well as private lives.

Third, strengthen the rule of law. The rule of law reigns when the actions and decisions of government are made and done in accordance with the Constitution and the laws, not pursuant to the wiles and whims of the governors.

Fourth, practice responsible citizenship by being constantly informed of national and community affairs, participating in communal activities, and helping in the transformation of our culture of patronage to a culture of integrity.

Fifth, adhere to and fortify democratic institutions like due process, civilian supremacy, and checks and balances.

VISIONARY LEADERSHIP BY EXAMPLE

Sixth, always start with a vision; have a clear idea of what you want to accomplish and the specific ways of accomplishing them. A vision-mission statement must be specific and quantifiable, not a mere collection of motherhood principles.

EPILOGUE

In those early three days as PLDT's new chief executive, Manuel V. Pangilinan led his subalterns in practising the prudent use of corporate assets, integrity in money matters and on-time office arrival. After a few years of leadership by example, the market worth of the Philippine Long Distance Telephone Company multiplied tenfold, and its corporate ethics a hundred fold.

Leadership by personal example of integrity, transparency and accountability, not by mere rhetoric and certainly not by double speak and hypocrisy, must top every innovative proposal to solve every organization's developmental problem and need. That, in a sentence, is my keynote message today. Maraming salamat po.

## Summary

Delivered April 9, 2008 as the keynote at the World Bank's Philippine Development Innovation Marketplace (Panibagong Paraan 2008, theme: Building Partnerships for Local Governance) -- invited by World Bank Country Director Bert Hofman -- this address by CJP opens with the CBCP's statement that the Philippines faces 'a crisis of truth and the pervading cancer of corruption,' with the Church calling for circles of discernment in parishes and Basic Ecclesial Communities. CJP links this to the Panibagong Paraan innovation: instead of the traditional top-down approach, solve governance problems from the base (local communities) going up to the apex -- 'this great innovation of solving the nation's problems from the base up is itself the big innovation in our marketplace today.' He presents six governance principles: (1) truth and integrity ('integrity is the moral courage to denounce a wrong and to promote the truth'); (2) transparency and accountability ('those who govern must endure the sunlight and scrutiny of their public and private lives'); (3) rule of law; (4) responsible citizenship (transforming 'a culture of patronage to a culture of integrity'); (5) democratic institutions (due process, civilian supremacy, checks and balances); (6) visionary leadership ('a vision-mission statement must be specific and quantifiable, not a mere collection of motherhood principles'). He closes with the MVP/PLDT three-day silent leadership story -- in this version with a quantified outcome for the first time: 'the market worth of PLDT multiplied tenfold, and its corporate ethics a hundred fold.' Final message: 'Leadership by personal example of integrity, transparency and accountability, not by mere rhetoric and certainly not by double speak and hypocrisy, must top every innovative proposal to solve every organization's developmental problem and need.'
