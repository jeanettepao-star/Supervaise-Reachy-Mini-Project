# How the Judiciary Can Help the Economy

Delivered November 29, 2012 at the ACCRA Law 40th Anniversary forum -- addressed to the Management Association of the Philippines, invited by Chairman Avelino V. Cruz -- this address by CJP opens with the Ave Cruz counterfactual ('he could have been Chief Justice a decade later... there would have been no Chief Justice Hilario G. Davide Jr. and Chief Justice Artemio V. Panganiban') and congratulates BPI President Aurelio Montinola III on being named MAP Management Man of the Year 2012 ('well deserved and, in fact, long overdue'). He describes the October 2006 Global Forum on Liberty and Prosperity (300+ jurists; Canadian CJ Beverley McLachlin suggested 'Under the Rule of Law' and 'fell in love with the Philippines, staying three more days after the Forum'). The speech's central answer: 'the judiciary must defer as much as possible to the political branches of government' on economic controversies -- empirically validated by the Philippines' best-in-ASEAN 2012 GDP growth (6.3% Q1, 5.9% Q2, 7.1% Q3; 6.5% for nine months; second in Asia after China). He presents three landmark decisions: Tanada v. Angara (May 1997 -- WTO upheld; 'what the Senate did was a valid exercise of its authority; whether it was wise is outside judicial inquiry'); La Bugal-B'Laan v. Ramos (original 8-5 struck down the Mining Law; MFR reversed it 10-4 with CJP as ponente -- 'The Constitution should be read in broad, life-giving strokes. It should not be used to strangulate economic growth'); and Gamboa v. Teves (June 2011 -- 60-40 rule limited to voting shares; CJP urges prospective application since foreign investors relied in good faith on the prior DOJ/SEC interpretation that the limit covers entire capital stock). He closes: 'In matters affecting the prosperity of our people, the judiciary can help the economy by deferring to the political branches of the government and to the private business sector which is the recognized engine of growth in a regime of free enterprise.'

## Notable Anecdotes

### Anecdote 1

Ave Cruz and the three non-existent CJs: 'After the EDSA people power revolution in 1986, President Corazon Aquino offered Ave a seat in the Supreme Court. However, he respectfully declined... Had he accepted President Cory Aquino's offer, he could have been Chief Justice a decade later. And there would have been no Chief Justice Hilario G. Davide Jr. and Chief Justice Artemio V. Panganiban.'

### Anecdote 2

CJ McLachlin fell in love with the Philippines: after the Global Forum she stayed three more days; 'she fell in love with the Philippines' -- prompting her suggestion to add 'Under the Rule of Law' to CJP's philosophy.

### Anecdote 3

Philippines best in ASEAN in 2012: 6.3% Q1, 5.9% Q2, 7.1% Q3; 6.5% overall -- 'best performer in the ASEAN Region and the second best in Asia, second only to China.' This is the empirical evidence CJP cites for why judicial deference works.

### Anecdote 4

La Bugal Constitution read in broad strokes: 'The Constitution should be read in broad, life-giving strokes. It should not be used to strangulate economic growth or to serve narrow, parochial interests. Rather, it should be construed to grant Congress and the President sufficient discretion and reasonable leeway to enable them to attract foreign investments and expertise.'
