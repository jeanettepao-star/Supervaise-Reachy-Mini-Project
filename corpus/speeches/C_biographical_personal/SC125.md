# Appreciation and Thanks

Transcript of the extemporaneous closing remarks delivered by retired Chief Justice Artemio V. Panganiban during the formal launching of the book A Test of Courage held at the Far Eastern University Auditorium, Manila on January 22, 2008.

---

Your Honors, my former colleagues in the Supreme Court; Your Excellencies, members of the diplomatic corps; other distinguished guests, ladies and gentlemen. May I say that Chief Justice Reynato S. Puno has written a letter expressing his inability to attend our launching today due to a prior commitment.

I am very happy to be back at Far Eastern University, the best school in the world, according to the FEU Alumni Association and me. Recently, an incumbent senator-friend wrote that graduates of the University of the Philippines constitute a majority of former Presidents of the Philippines, cabinet members, senators, congressmen and even justices of the Supreme Court. If that were so, how come the Philippines is still plagued by rampant corruption, unmitigated violence, grinding poverty, and extralegal killings? Aha, because not all the UP graduates became FEU deans, like Sen. Salonga.

Levity aside, after hearing our esteemed guest speaker, Justice Antonio T. Carpio, and the book authors, Sen. Jovito R. Salonga and Mrs. Evelyn Miranda-Feliciano, what can I really say except thank you and maraming salamat po for your kindness.

Thank you to the generous chairperson of the Far Eastern University, Dr. LOURDES REYES MONTINOLA, who, according to Forbes Magazine, is among the 40 richest Filipinos. But who has risked her fortune, time, effort and name in elevating the FEU to new educational heights. I met Dr. Montinola when I was still a student leader in FEU and she was the corporate secretary of the University. Maraming salamat po Chairperson Montinola for making us all proud to be Tamaraws.

Salamat po Dr. LYDIA B. ECHAUZ, the dynamo partner of Dr. Montinola in energizing and electrifying the deans, faculty and students of FEU. She put to intelligent and maximum use the resources provided by the FEU Board of Trustees in parlaying the FEU into what it is today, on its 80th founding anniversary.

Salamat po Dr. RAUL C. PANGALANGAN, chair of Bantay Katarungan, former UP law dean, favorite exchange professor of Harvard Law School. That he has not been appointed to the Supreme Court yet is not a reflection of his lack of credentials for the job; rather, it reflects the lack of ability of the appointing authority to recognize talent, intellectual gravitas, and courage in a truly outstanding lawyer and academic.

Salamant po my COLLEAGUES IN THE PANGANIBAN COURT. I give special tribute and thanks to Justices Conchita Carpio Morales, Adolfo S. Azcuna, Angelina Sandoval-Gutierrez, and of course our guest speaker, Justice Antonio T. Carpio.

My most profound thanks go to the authors, Mrs. EVELYN MIRANDA FELICIANO and former Senate President JOVITO R. SALONGA. I first met Senate President Salonga more than fifty years ago when he was my professor and dean at the FEU Institute of Law. Since then up to the present, he has become my lifetime mentor and guru not only in the labyrinths of the law but more important, on how to enjoy a life worth living.

For this undeserved honor, I can only say Maraming, maraming salamat po, Senate President Salonga at sa inyong lahat na naririto ngayon sa FEU Auditorium. Mabuhay po kayong lahat.

## Summary

Delivered January 22, 2008 as CJP's response remarks at an FEU 80th anniversary book launch -- after Justice Antonio Carpio's lecture and the book presentations by Senator Jovito Salonga and Mrs. Evelyn Miranda-Feliciano; CJ Puno sent an apology letter -- this address is a sequence of individual tributes punctuated by wit. It opens with the claim that FEU is 'the best school in the world, according to the FEU Alumni Association and me,' and the UP-vs-FEU joke: since UP produces most Philippine presidents, cabinet members and justices, why is the country still plagued by corruption and poverty? 'Aha, because not all the UP graduates became FEU deans, like Sen. Salonga.' CJP thanks FEU Chair Dr. Lourdes Reyes Montinola (Forbes 40 richest Filipinos; met her when she was corporate secretary and he was a student leader; 'has risked her fortune, time, effort and name in elevating FEU to new educational heights'); FEU President Dr. Lydia Echauz ('the dynamo partner of Dr. Montinola in energizing and electrifying the deans, faculty and students'); Dr. Raul Pangalangan (Bantay Katarungan chair; former UP law dean; Harvard's favorite exchange professor; 'that he has not been appointed to the Supreme Court yet is not a reflection of his lack of credentials; rather, it reflects the lack of ability of the appointing authority to recognize talent, intellectual gravitas, and courage'); Panganiban Court colleagues Justices Conchita Carpio Morales, Adolfo Azcuna, Angelina Sandoval-Gutierrez and Antonio Carpio; Mrs. Miranda-Feliciano; and Senator Salonga: 'I first met him more than fifty years ago when he was my professor and dean at FEU. Since then he has become my lifetime mentor and guru not only in the labyrinths of the law but more important, on how to enjoy a life worth living.'
