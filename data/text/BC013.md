# FAITH

## Faith Brought Me to the Supreme Court

My brothers and sisters in Christ; you all know that I was recently appointed Justice of the Supreme Court of the Philippines. I took my oath only a week ago. Let me now tell you how God’s Word guided me in my journey to the Court, how He humbled me in the process and how, eventually, He rewarded me. Let me also tell you how my prayers and faith in Jesus Christ combined to lead me to victory and fulfillment.

Being elevated to the Supreme Court is the highest aspiration and dream of every lawyer. Like any lawyer, I thus aspired to have a place in this Court. My serious quest began some three years ago. 

The formal process of getting into the highest court begins in the Judicial and Bar Council or JBC. This council is empowered by our Constitution to screen and then recommend a list of nominees from which the President of the Philippines eventually chooses the appointee. Once appointed, the justice holds office during good behavior until he reaches the age of seventy. The screening process is rather rigid - man’s personal life, professional career and lifelong activities are minutely scrutinized. 
	From 1992 to 1994, at least seven vacancies in the Supreme Court were created due to the retirement of incumbent members. In each of these vacancies, my name was submitted to the JBC for screening. But in all seven, I failed the test. Many objections were raised against me - the alleged insufficiency of my professional competence and my alleged sins and wrongdoings. At one time, a former client suddenly surfaced to complain about my alleged mishandling of a case that happened 25 years ago. And after I was able to answer and explain the charges against me, I was bypassed anyway because career appellate justices were given priority over practising lawyers. It was also pointed out that I might be qualified, but that there were others who were even more qualified. In other words, these past three years of screening were one long story of frustration and defeat. Many times, I would sob and ask the Lord: "How long, O Lord, must I bear this frustration and embarrassment of being rejected? Why must I suffer for charges and accusations which are absolutely baseless and misleading? I have repented my sins and reformed my life; why do You still allow me to be humiliated and chastised?" 

In all these instances, however, and in spite of such hard rhetorical questions I asked the Lord, I never lost faith in His wisdom and providence. I always took comfort and consolation in Romans 8:28: 

God makes all things work together 

for the good of those who love Him and are 

called according to His purpose.

In January 1995, after having been bypassed seven times, and convinced that the Lord did not want me to be in the Supreme Court, I humbled myself and bowed to what I thought was God's will for me. My spirit was shattered and my heart bled, but I uplifted my ambition to God with a solemn prayer:

Take, O Lord, all that I am, all that I will ever be. Take my personal ambition, my liberty, my career. Do what You will with them and I will follow whatever it is You want me to do. 

In July of the same year, another vacancy in Court occurred with the retirement of Justice Camilo Quiason. A close friend who was and is still a member of the High Court asked me, "Why don't you try again? Don't pass up the opportunity because the next vacancy will be in late 1997 already." At first, I did not take my friend's suggestion seriously, because at the time I had already given up my ambition. 

However, about 1 a.m. on Saturday, July 29, 1995, I woke up and could not sleep. My friend's words kept bothering me, kept reverberating in my ears. So I decided to pray for over two hours. I told the Lord that I had already surrendered my quest to Him, that I had already given up. Why was I still bothered? I fell asleep about 4a.m., waiting for a sign from the Lord. None came that night. 

But the following morning, he spoke loud and clear to me. Let me explain. 

Brothers and sisters, it is my faith that God speaks to us through the Bible. Though written some two thousand years ago, the Scriptures are still sources of God's personal messages. For Jesus Christ is the same today, yesterday and forever. I believe that if we read or listen intently and prayerfully — in full faith and confidence — God will speak to us personally as we are struck by a verse in the Good Book that comes alive and answers our questions. 

The following morning, Sunday, July 30, 1995, I served as a special minister of Holy Communion in our parish. As was my habit, with eyes closed and with an ascending Jesus in my mental vision, I intently listened to our parish priest as he read the gospel for the day. I was struck by the following verses he read from the gospel according to Luke 11:1-11: 

Ask and you shall receive, seek and you shall find, knock and the door shall be opened to you. For whoever asks, receives; whoever seeks, finds; whoever knocks, is admitted. 

I had cold perspiration and my body hair stood up as I gasped, "My Lord, are you asking me to persist in my quest?" But I was not satisfied. I told God in my prayer, "Lord, I may just be deluding myself, hearing things I want to hear. Please give me another sign to confirm this message." 

The Lord's confirmation came after one week in the same Church as the same priest was reciting the gospel reading. The following Sunday, August 6, the reading was on the transfiguration of our Lord Jesus as related in Luke 9:28-36. The gospel relates that as our Lord was transfigured, 

. . . a cloud came and overshadowed them and the disciples grew fearful. . . . Then from the cloud came a voice which said, "This is my Son, my Chosen One. Listen to Him." 

"Listen to Him . . . " These three words struck me And once again my heart pounded madly as my body hair stood up and cold sweat oozed from all over my skin. I became sure of what the Lord wanted me to do. After all, it was our Lord Jesus who said the week before, "Ask and you shall receive, seek and you shall find, knock and the door shall be opened to you." And now our Heavenly Father commanded me: "Listen to Him!" So I followed Him. I submitted my name again to the JBC for screening. 

I felt so sure of the Lord's two messages for me that I shared them with my family. On August 9, Wednesday, Leni (my wife) and I were prayer leaders during the prayer meeting of Bukas Loob ng Diyos (South Sector) held in St. James School in Sucat, Paranaque. During my reflection in that prayer meeting, I spoke of God's two messages for me even at the risk of being ridiculed should the discernment turn out to be false. I boldly proclaimed my discernment and publicly claimed God's will for me. I declared my determination to pursue God's will in my career by quoting a favorite Jesuit saying: 

I will work as if everything depended on me, and I will pray as if everything depended on God. 

This did not mean, however, that my trials and tests were over. I had to undergo more of them. But I clung to God's word and never lost my faith in His promise. Let me continue my story. 

During the meeting of the JBC on September 13, there was a serious attempt to exclude me from the list. I would have been excluded from right then, had it not been for one member, who I believe was guided by the Holy Spirit. He pleaded: "I think we should let Panganiban's name stay in the list in the meantime. We are meeting next week anyway and, if you still insist on deleting him, so be it." 

This member told me two days later that, unless a miracle happened, I would surely be out of the list. So I prayed again and told the Lord that human forces, however exalted and powerful, could not prevail against His will. I reaffirmed my full faith in and dependence on His word. 

And, true enough, during the JBC meeting one week thereafter on September 20, no one, by some mysterious reason, objected to my name, and for the first time in over three years, I passed the screening. A miracle! 

Thereafter, a list of the five JBC recommendees including my name was sent to the President of the Philippines. After being informed that finally I was on the JBC list, President Ramos called and asked me pointblank, "How did you do it?" "Do what, sir?" I asked back. "How did you get the JBC to nominate you? How did you do it?" he repeated.

"Well, sir," I replied, "I did not do it. The Lord did it!" 

It took the President another two weeks to make his appointment known. All this time, I had asked our Community during all our prayer meetings and during all intercessory sessions — and Leni asked the Carmelites and the Pink Sisters - to storm the heavens with supplications and sacrifices. And not just I but also Leni and my whole family clung to God's discern ed word and never doubted that He would be faithful and would make His will prevail.

Exactly two weeks after the JBC included me in the list of nominees, the President called me by phone, saying in effect, "I am the Lord's instrument. He has chosen you, and I therefore appoint you justice of the Supreme Court." 

I answered, "Alleluia, praise be the Lord God Almighty." 

Since then, I have never ceased to praise and thank God for this display of His might and power in my career. 

Our God took His time because He wanted me to learn some lessons in humility and purification - to acknowledge my powerlessness. He wanted me to recognize His power over all things, to cling to Him, to have steadfast faith in Him. He wanted me to know that on my own, I could do nothing; but with Him, I can do anything. 

Truly, brothers and sisters, when we discern His will, when we surrender our ambition to Him, when we show our obedience and faith - I repeat, OBEDIENCE AND FAITH - He responds mightily in His own time. And because it is my faith that it was God who made this appointment possible, I hereby declare before this Assembly that all my actions and all my decisions will only be in accordance with His commandments and His gospel. I hold office by God's grace and I pledge to serve Him and our people with fortitude, integrity, competence and prudence. I dedicate all that I am, all that I will ever be to Him who is the Source of everything. 

Praise to You, Lord Jesus Christ.
