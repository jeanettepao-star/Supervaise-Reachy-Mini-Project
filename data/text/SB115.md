A PRINCIPLED CONGLOMERATE

Date:       2008-10-22

Publisher:  cjpanganiban.com

Source:     https://cjpanganiban.com/new-paradigms-of-justice-and-education-2/

Remarks delivered by retired Chief Justice Artemio V. Panganiban on October 22, 2008 during the two-day In Sync: Living Excellence and Social Responsibility the Lopez Way Seminar held at the Eugenio Lopez Center, Antipolo, Rizal.

---

On behalf of the members of the boards of directors and other officials of the Lopez Group attending this two-day seminar on Living Excellence and Social Responsibility the Lopez Way, I thank the organizers for this opportunity to reflect on the values and principles that guide our conglomerate.

The Lopez Group is one of the most storied business families in Philippine history. Its patriarch, Don Eugenio Lopez Sr., built a business empire that now encompasses some of the most vital industries in our country, from energy to media to real estate. Through several generations, the Lopez family has demonstrated that private enterprise and public responsibility can and must go together.

EXCELLENCE AND SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

The twin themes of this seminar, excellence and social responsibility, are at the heart of what makes a conglomerate principled. Excellence means doing everything we do at the highest possible standard. It means striving for quality in every product we deliver, every service we provide, every decision we make. But excellence alone is not enough. Excellence without social responsibility becomes mere profit-seeking at the expense of the community.

Social responsibility, on the other hand, means recognizing that businesses exist not just to make money but to serve a larger purpose. We have responsibilities to our employees, to our customers, to our communities, and to our country. The Lopez Group has long embraced this principle. Its investments in energy, particularly in power generation and distribution, serve the basic needs of millions of Filipinos. Its media properties help shape public opinion and serve the democratic ideals of free expression and access to information.

LIBERTY AND PROSPERITY UNDER THE RULE OF LAW

As a retired jurist and as someone who has spent his career thinking about the relationship between law and society, I see the work of principled conglomerates like the Lopez Group as deeply connected to my own advocacy of liberty and prosperity under the rule of law. Businesses that operate with integrity, that pay their taxes, that respect their workers' rights, that tell the truth in their public communications, are not just good corporate citizens. They are pillars of a democratic society.

Conversely, businesses that evade taxes, that exploit their workers, that engage in corruption and bribery, undermine not just their own long-term interests but the fabric of our democratic institutions. The rule of law cannot survive if the powerful systematically circumvent it.

A PERSONAL NOTE

I am privileged to have been invited to serve as an independent director of First Philippine Holdings Corporation and later of Meralco, both flagship companies of the Lopez Group. In my experience, the Lopez Group is genuinely committed to the principles it espouses. It is not perfect, no organization is, but it strives in good faith to live up to its stated values.

I commend the organizers of this seminar for taking time away from the daily pressures of business to reflect on these fundamental questions of purpose and value. I hope that the insights you gain here will strengthen your commitment to being not just a profitable conglomerate but a principled one, worthy of the trust of the Filipino people.

Maraming salamat po.
