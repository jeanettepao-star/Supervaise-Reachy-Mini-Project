AFTER THE LAST SONA, GMA****'****S OPTIONS

Date:       2009-07-30

Publisher:  cjpanganiban.com

Source:     https://cjpanganiban.com/after-the-last-sona-gmas-options/

Outline of the extemporaneous talk delivered by retired Chief Justice Artemio V. Panganiban during the Inquirer Briefing on July 30, 2009 at the Philippine Daily Inquirer Multi-Purpose Hall, Makati City.

---

1. Prepare to step down from power

Exit gracefully, name transition team and relish legacy. Some SONA bites: The state of our nation is a strong economy. Our reforms gave us the resources to protect our people, our financial system and our economy from the worst of shocks. Since 2001 our economy posted uninterrupted growth for 33 quarters; more than double its size from $76 billion to $186 billion. The average GDP growth from 2001 to the first quarter of 2009 is the highest in 43 years. I never expressed the desire to extend myself beyond my term. I accepted the invitation of President Obama to be the first Southeast Asian leader to meet him at the White House later this week.

Assure a credible 2010 election. Protect her back; strengthen hold on the House of Representatives, Supreme Court and Sandiganbayan, Ombudsman, Commission on Elections, and Military. Choose a viable presidential candidate who would shield her; run for vice-president and team up with Noli or Erap; run for a congressional seat in Pampanga.

2. Extend reign as President/Prime Minister

Charter change via peoples initiative for simple amendments only, not complicated revisions; or via constituent assembly requiring a three-fourths vote of all members of Congress; or via constitutional convention, with no more time before elections.

Martial law: to quell invasion or rebellion for 60 days only; may be revoked, shortened or extended by majority vote of all members of Congress; SC can pass upon the sufficiency of the factual basis; SC must decide within 30 days.

Emergency rule: no constitutional authority; no international backing; our people will rebel against it; even the military may not support it.

Holdover: no election due to man-made or natural causes; no proclamation due to election held but no winner chosen due to automation problems; succession to the presidency via VP, Senate President, Speaker.

3. Preventions and alternatives

Honest, orderly and peaceful elections in 2010 (HOPE), preferably automated. Vigilant people and media. People power. CBCP and communal action. Twitter revolt. Full-scale revolution.
